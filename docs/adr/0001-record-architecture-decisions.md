# 1. Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

Date: 2026-09-22

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The repository requires structured tracking of architectural and design decisions for the `nf-artic` Nextflow workflow.

## Decision

We will document architectural decisions as numbered markdown files in `docs/adr/`.

## Consequences

Major design changes, containerization updates, and algorithm migrations (such as Medaka to Clair3) will be recorded here for future reference.
